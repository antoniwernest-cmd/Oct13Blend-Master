BLEND MASTER

Open index.html, or upload this complete Blend-Master folder to a static web host. Keep index.html, all JavaScript/CSS files and assets together. No build command is required. No ZIP or PDF is supplied.

CHOOSE YOUR SCHOOL SUPPLY
The entry page follows Spelling Master's design. Choose one of 20 school-supply pictures. Use the same object to resume that child's work. No name, password or student ID is needed. The object appears during activities and on every assessment row. On a shared browser, give each child a different object.

LEVELS
A, B, C: one cloze blank with exactly three choices. Each level mixes initial-blend blanks with whole-word blanks. Sentences increase in length from A to C.
AA: two blanks; BB: three blanks; CC: four blanks. Each question has exactly five choices. Each combines at least one initial blend with whole words that do not begin with an initial consonant blend.
All six levels contain 162 activities: 972 activities in total. The level labels are A, B, C, AA, BB, CC.

READ, DRAG OR TAP
Listen reads the full target sentence using the device's built-in English voice. Stop cancels speech. Voice speed can be changed. If a browser blocks automatic speech, tap Listen. No speech is sent to a server by this application.
For a blend blank, the rest of the word stays visible: for example, __ock accepts cl to make clock. A whole-word blank accepts a complete word from the choices. The blend and remaining letters stay together on a line.
Drag a choice directly onto any blank. Or tap a blank, then a push-button choice. Keyboard users can Tab to a blank and press Enter, then Tab to a choice and press Enter. Every blank is a real button.
Choices are reusable and stay in the bank. The selected blank is highlighted, and its label says whether a blend or whole word is needed. A correct choice moves the selection to the next unfinished blank. Wrong choices remain visible so the student can revise them.
Choice text is grey in the bank. Placed text is black in the notebook. Clear this blank removes only the selected answer. Reset question clears the current answers, hides hints and stops speech, while keeping all assessment history.
Check my answer checks the whole sentence after every blank has been filled. Students may hear the sentence again, revise an answer, or use Show me a hint. The hint reveals the full sentence and is recorded separately.

NOTEBOOK AND TABLETS
The activity uses the Spelling Master notebook design, red margin lines and pencil. The date follows the student's current local device date in the format Tuesday October 13th, 2026, and refreshes at midnight and on return to the page. Text is upright Century Gothic, with a sans-serif fallback if the font is not installed.
Finger, stylus and mouse dragging use Pointer Events. Pull-to-refresh gestures are suppressed in the activity; the teacher menu can scroll normally. Browser toolbar refresh and operating-system controls remain under the browser's control. Saved work is restored after a reload.
Spacing adapts to the screen height to keep the question and controls together. Unusually small screens may scroll to keep the words and touch targets usable. Actual tablet rendering has not been visually verified because local browser preview is blocked in this session.

EXCEL ASSESSMENT
Teacher tools downloads an actual .xlsx workbook for the current object. The entry page can download all 20 objects. The workbook contains Summary, Progress, Blanks, Attempts and Guide sheets.
Every choice tap or drop into a blank records the time, object, level, activity, blank, blend/word type, offered choices, student choice, correctness, current sentence and input method. Sentence checks are counted separately. The Blanks sheet shows each blank's current answer and choice attempts. Hints, listens, active time and teacher support ratings are also included.
Completed means every blank was correct together at least once. Later practice or Reset question keeps that achievement and earlier attempts. The separate Reset this object's progress action in Teacher tools requires confirmation and deletes only that object's Blend Master progress.
Saved progress is local to this browser and device. It is separate from Spelling Master and Mystic/Magic Cat. Export Excel before clearing browser data, changing devices or changing site address. An Excel download is a record, not a restore file.

PICTURES
There are 162 native picture crops from the nine supplied blend posters. Printed answer labels and blend headings are excluded. Words printed inside the stop sign, glue bottle and snack bag are covered, as are the floating sleep letters. Covers stay aligned when the picture changes size. Crops keep their original resolution and are not enlarged beyond their native width in the activity.
The crow and window are named to match their pictures rather than the mistaken printed labels. The purple fruit is called a plum. S'more keeps its apostrophe and is used as a whole word. Full sentences describe the supplied pictures; related whole-word blanks use words from those sentences.
